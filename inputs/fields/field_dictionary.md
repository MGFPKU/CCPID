## Mitigation relevance

- Field name: Mitigation relevance
- Attribute type: Policy description
- Definition: Indicates whether the policy instrument is a climate change mitigation one or a mitigation-relevant one. "Direct" means it is a mitigation instrument which explicitly intends to reduce or remove GHG emissions. "Indirect" means it is a mitigation-relevant instrument which may induce changes in emissions (positive or negative) without necessarily having emission reductions or removals as a primary goal.
- Allowed values:
  - Direct
  - Indirect
- Examples:
  - Not specified in source workbook.

  - Entry type: Categorial
